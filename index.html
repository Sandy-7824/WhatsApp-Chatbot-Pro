Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about integration, pricing, or capabilities? Find answers below.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account?

Yes. We assist you directly in setting up the official WhatsApp Cloud API through your Meta Business Manager. This ensures your account remains compliant with WhatsApp's guidelines and prevents number suspension.

Can my human support agents takeover chats?

Absolutely. The platform features an integrated live inbox interface. Whenever the chatbot receives a request that requires human attention or when a customer clicks "Talk to Agent", it routes the conversation seamlessly to your team.

Is my customer conversation data secure?

Yes, security is our top priority. All communications are fully encrypted in transit. We are fully compliant with GDPR regulations, and we never resell or distribute any customer information or metadata.

Can I connect the chatbot with my CRMs?

Yes, we offer native, seamless integration options with major CRM and ticketing suites like HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, and Zapier, permitting frictionless lead updates and database synching.

Is there a setup fee for custom builds?

Our standard packages feature self-service setup wizards. However, if your enterprise requires customized chatbot integrations, complex CRM flows, or personalized NLP tuning, our team can deliver a tailored setup for an additional fee.
